Quick Beef Tacos

Description

A quick and flavorful weeknight dinner option loaded with seasoned ground beef and your favorite taco toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 package taco shells
  • Shredded lettuce
  • Diced tomatoes
  • Shredded cheddar cheese
  • Salsa

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat until completely browned; drain excess grease.
  2. Stir in the taco seasoning and water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Warm the taco shells according to the package instructions.
  4. Spoon the seasoned beef into the taco shells.
  5. Top with lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, and salsa. Serve immediately.
