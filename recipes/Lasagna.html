Lasagna

Description

This is the world's best lasagna recipe. It takes a little time to make, but it is completely worth it for the rich, cheesy, and meaty flavor!

Ingredients

  • 1 pound sweet Italian sausage
  • 3/4 pound lean ground beef
  • 1/2 cup minced onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Make the meat sauce by cooking the sausage, ground beef, onion, and garlic until browned. Stir in the tomatoes and simmer.
  2. Boil the lasagna noodles in a pot of salted water until al dente.
  3. Mix the ricotta cheese with eggs and seasonings.
  4. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella cheese in a baking dish.
  5. Bake at 375 degrees F (190 degrees C) for about 50 minutes until bubbly and golden brown.
